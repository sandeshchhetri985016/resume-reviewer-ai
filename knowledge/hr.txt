# Human Resources Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on HR resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Hiring metrics (e.g., "Filled 50+ positions annually with average time-to-hire of 18 days")
- Employee retention and engagement data (e.g., "Reduced turnover from 25% to 15% over 2 years")
- HRIS/platform proficiency (Workday, SuccessFactors, ADP, BambooHR, Employment Hero)
- Policy development and implementation examples
- IR/ER case management experience with outcomes
- Training and development program results

## Metrics That Impress HR Leaders
- Time-to-hire and cost-per-hire reductions
- Retention rate improvements
- Employee engagement score improvements
- Training completion rates and effectiveness
- Compliance audit results
- Number of recruitment campaigns managed

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Recruitment & Talent Acquisition
- Sourcing strategy and channel effectiveness
- Interview process design and improvement
- Employer branding initiatives
- Onboarding program design and feedback
- Diversity and inclusion hiring metrics

### Employee Relations & Compliance
- Award interpretation and Fair Work compliance
- Performance management case handling
- Investigation and grievance resolution
- Policy writing and implementation
- WHS/OHS knowledge and incident management

### HR Operations
- HRIS implementation or migration
- Payroll processing and accuracy
- Reporting and workforce analytics
- Budget management for HR initiatives

## Common Resume Mistakes in HR
- People metrics without context or comparison
- No mention of HRIS or platform experience
- Vague about policy contributions or authorship
- Missing IR/ER case examples or outcomes
- Compliance knowledge implied but not demonstrated
- No evidence of stakeholder or leadership communication

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, HR areas, industry)
2. Technical Skills (HRIS, Platforms, Tools)
3. Professional Experience (scope: headcount, locations, team size)
4. Education (HR degree, relevant qualifications)
5. Certifications (AHRI, SHRM, CIPD, Diploma of HR)
6. Additional: Volunteer HR work, committee memberships
