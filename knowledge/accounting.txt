# Accounting & Finance Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on accounting resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Professional certifications (CPA, CA, IPA, ACCA, CFA) with membership numbers
- Software proficiency (Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB, SAP, Oracle)
- Month-end/year-end close process experience and timelines managed
- Audit experience with specific findings and outcomes
- Process improvement metrics (e.g., "Reduced month-end close from 10 to 5 days")
- Stakeholder communication and cross-department collaboration

## Metrics That Impress Accounting Managers
- Time saved through process automation (e.g., "Automated AP process saving 20 hrs/week")
- Error reduction percentages in financial reporting
- Number of entities or ledgers managed
- Reconciliation completion rates and timeliness
- Budget variance analysis accuracy
- Cost savings identified through analysis

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Technical Skills
- Accounting standards: GAAP, IFRS, AASB, SOX compliance
- Tax knowledge: BAS, GST, PAYG, corporate tax, FBT
- Financial reporting: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, variance analysis
- Software: Excel (advanced), ERP systems, accounting platforms

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail and accuracy focus
- Deadline management (especially month-end, year-end)
- Communication with non-finance stakeholders
- Ethics and confidentiality handling

## Common Resume Mistakes in Accounting
- Listing software without proficiency level
- No mention of reporting timelines or deadlines met
- Missing certification status or membership numbers
- Vague descriptions like "assisted with" or "supported"
- No examples of process improvements or efficiency gains
- Failing to mention audit experience or outcomes

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, qualifications, industries served)
2. Technical Skills (Software, Standards, Specialisations)
3. Professional Experience (with scope: entities, team size, reporting lines)
4. Education (degree, institution, graduation year)
5. Certifications & Professional Memberships (membership numbers)
6. Additional: Languages, volunteer accounting work
