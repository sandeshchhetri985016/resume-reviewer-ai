# Aged Care Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on aged care resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Resident-to-staff ratios managed (e.g., "Cared for 25 residents on morning shift")
- Dementia and palliative care experience with specific training mentioned
- Compliance with Aged Care Quality Standards
- ACFI/AN-ACC documentation experience
- Incident reduction metrics (falls, pressure injuries, behaviour incidents)
- Team leadership and shift coordination experience

## Metrics That Impress Aged Care Employers
- Number of residents under care
- Compliance audit scores
- Incident reduction percentages
- Family satisfaction feedback improvements
- Training delivered to care staff
- Medication error rates reduced

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years in aged care, specialisations like dementia/palliative)
2. Core Competencies (clinical skills, communication, documentation)
3. Experience (with resident numbers, team sizes, and quality outcomes)
4. Certifications (Aged Care Cert III/IV, First Aid, CPR, Manual Handling)
5. Additional: Languages, availability for shifts
