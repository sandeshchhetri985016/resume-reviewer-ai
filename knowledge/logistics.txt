# Logistics & Supply Chain Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on logistics and supply chain resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Volume metrics (e.g., "Managed warehouse operations handling 10,000+ orders per week")
- Cost reduction achievements (e.g., "Reduced freight costs by 18% through carrier consolidation")
- System proficiency (SAP, Oracle SCM, WMS, TMS, Cin7, NetSuite)
- Inventory accuracy and improvement (e.g., "Improved inventory accuracy from 85% to 99%")
- Team leadership (e.g., "Supervised team of 25+ warehouse staff across 3 shifts")
- Process improvement projects with measurable outcomes

## Metrics That Impress Logistics Managers
- On-time delivery rates and improvements
- Cost per unit shipped or cost per kilometer
- Inventory turnover and accuracy percentages
- Warehouse throughput (orders/hour, picks/hour)
- Lead time reduction achievements
- Customer satisfaction scores for logistics
- Fleet utilization and route optimization results

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Operations & Warehouse
- Receiving, putaway, picking, packing, dispatch processes
- Warehouse layout optimization and slotting
- WMS implementation or migration experience
- Safety compliance in warehouse environment
- Forklift, reach truck, order picker operations

### Transport & Distribution
- Fleet management and route planning
- Carrier negotiation and relationship management
- Freight forwarding (air, sea, road, rail)
- Customs and international shipping documentation
- Dangerous goods handling certification

### Supply Chain Management
- Demand forecasting and inventory planning
- Supplier management and procurement
- Cross-functional coordination (purchasing, sales, production)
- Continuous improvement (Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen)
- Risk management and contingency planning

## Common Resume Mistakes in Logistics
- Volume or throughput numbers not mentioned
- Cost savings not quantified
- System names omitted or vague ("WMS experience" without naming which one)
- No safety record or incident data
- Missing team size or scope of responsibility
- Inventory accuracy not reported

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, sector, key achievements)
2. Technical Skills (Systems, Equipment, Methodologies)
3. Professional Experience (volume, team size, budget, outcomes per role)
4. Education (degree, relevant certifications)
5. Certifications (Six Sigma, CPIM, CSCP, Dangerous Goods, Forklift License)
6. Additional: Safety record, awards, system implementations
