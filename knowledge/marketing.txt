# Marketing Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on marketing resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Campaign performance metrics (e.g., "Generated 500+ qualified leads from LinkedIn campaign with 12% conversion rate")
- ROI attribution (e.g., "Achieved 4.5x ROAS on Google Ads campaign")
- Platform expertise (HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Analytics, Mailchimp, SEMrush)
- Content marketing examples with engagement and conversion data
- A/B testing results and optimization cycles
- Channel mix experience (SEO, SEM, social, email, content, paid)

## Metrics That Impress Marketing Directors
- Traffic growth (MoM/YoY), lead generation numbers
- Conversion rate improvements through optimization
- Cost per acquisition (CPA) reduction
- ROI and ROAS on marketing spend
- Email open rates, click-through rates, list growth
- Social media engagement rates and follower growth
- Marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) generated

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Digital Marketing
- SEO: keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlink profiles
- SEM: Google Ads, Bing Ads, campaign optimization
- Social: organic and paid strategy across platforms
- Email: segmentation, automation flows, A/B testing
- Content: blog, video, whitepapers, case studies

### Marketing Operations
- Marketing automation tools (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot)
- CRM integration and lead scoring
- Analytics and attribution modeling
- Budget management and forecasting
- Agency and vendor management

### Strategic Skills
- Brand positioning and messaging
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Customer journey mapping
- Campaign strategy and go-to-market planning

## Common Resume Mistakes in Marketing
- Vanity metrics without context (e.g., "10K followers" without engagement rate)
- No ROI or conversion data — only activity descriptions
- Vague about specific role in team projects
- Missing platform names or tools used
- No mention of budget managed or campaign spend
- Failing to show progression in scope or responsibility

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, channels, industries)
2. Technical Skills (Platforms, Tools, Analytics)
3. Professional Experience (channel, budget, metrics per role)
4. Education (degree, relevant certifications)
5. Certifications (Google Ads, HubSpot, Salesforce, Meta Blueprint)
6. Portfolio (campaign examples, case studies, links)
