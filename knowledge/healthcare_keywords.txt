# Healthcare Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on healthcare resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Patient ratios and caseload numbers (e.g., "Managed 15+ clients daily")
- Compliance rates (e.g., "100% compliance with Aged Care Quality Standards")
- Incident reduction metrics (e.g., "Reduced falls by 35% through improved mobility programs")
- Certification currency (First Aid, CPR, Manual Handling, NDIS screening)
- Person-centred care language and client advocacy examples

## Metrics That Impress Healthcare Recruiters
- Patient-to-staff ratios managed
- Number of care plans developed or reviewed
- Incident reduction percentages
- Compliance audit scores (e.g., "98% compliance in unannounced audit")
- Client satisfaction scores or improvements
- Team supervision numbers (e.g., "Supervised team of 8 support workers")
- Training delivered (e.g., "Trained 20 staff on manual handling protocols")

## Critical Keywords to Check for
### Aged Care
- ACAT assessment, ACFI, AN-ACC, Home Care Packages, CHSP
- Dementia care, palliative care, falls prevention
- Mealtime management, dysphagia, texture-modified foods

### Disability Support
- NDIS, SIL, STA, SDA, ILO, Positive Behaviour Support
- Person-centred approach, independence promotion
- Community access, social support, daily living skills

### Clinical Skills
- Medication administration, wound care, catheter care
- Vital signs, BGL monitoring, pain assessment
- Manual handling, hoist operation, transfers

## Common Resume Mistakes in Healthcare
- Listing duties without context (e.g., "Provided personal care" → should specify scope and impact)
- Missing certification details including expiry dates
- No indication of regulatory knowledge (Aged Care Act, NDIS Code of Conduct)
- Failing to mention challenging behaviours or complex care experience
- Omitting supervision or team leadership experience
- Using passive language instead of action verbs

## Certifications & Licenses
Check for these mandatory credentials per role:
- Personal Care Worker: Certificate III in Individual Support, First Aid, CPR
- Disability Support: NDIS Worker Screening, Manual Handling, Medication cert
- Enrolled Nurse: AHPRA registration, Medication Endorsement
- Registered Nurse: AHPRA registration, relevant postgraduate qualifications
- All roles: Working with Children Check, Police Check, COVID-19 training

## Resume Structure Recommendations
Healthcare resumes should include:
1. Professional Summary (2-4 sentences with years experience and specialisation)
2. Core Competencies (bullet list of 8-12 clinical and personal care skills)
3. Professional Experience (reverse chronological with quantified achievements)
4. Education (qualifications with institution and completion dates)
5. Certifications & Licenses (name, issuer, expiry — critical for this field)
6. Additional: Languages, Volunteer Work if applicable
