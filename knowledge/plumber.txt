# Plumber Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on plumber resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Licenses and tickets with expiry dates (Plumbing License, Gas Fitting License, White Card)
- Project scope (e.g., "Managed plumbing installation for 50-unit apartment complex")
- Safety record (e.g., "Zero safety incidents over 8 years in commercial plumbing")
- Supervision and apprentice mentoring (e.g., "Supervised 2 apprentices across multiple residential builds")
- Specialisation areas (domestic, commercial, industrial, gas, drainage, roofing)
- Backflow testing and certifications with results

## Metrics That Impress Plumbing Contractors
- Number of projects completed (new builds, renovations, maintenance contracts)
- Team size supervised
- Job completion rates on time and budget
- Safety incident record
- Client retention or repeat business
- Complex installation examples (hot water systems, sewerage infrastructure)

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Licenses & Tickets
- Plumbing License (with endorsement level)
- Gas Fitting License (Class A, B)
- Drainage License
- Water Supply License
- Backflow Prevention Certification (testing and installation)
- White Card / Construction Induction
- High Risk Work License (EWP, forklift if applicable)
- First Aid, CPR, Working at Heights, Confined Space
- Refrigerant Handling License (if relevant)

### Experience Types
- Domestic: new builds, renovations, bathroom renovations, hot water systems
- Commercial: fit-outs, sanitary plumbing, commercial gas fitting
- Industrial: process piping, compressed air, steam systems
- Roofing: gutters, downpipes, stormwater, roof sheeting
- Drainage: sewer, stormwater, excavation, trenching
- Maintenance: fault finding, reactive repairs, preventative maintenance

### Technical Skills
- Pipe types: copper, PVC, PEX, poly, galvanized
- Welding: oxy, MIG, TIG for plumbing applications
- Gas appliance installation and testing
- Backflow testing and certification
- Thermostatic mixing valve installation
- Sewer camera inspection
- Blueprint and plan reading

## Common Resume Mistakes for Plumbers
- License number or expiry date missing
- No project examples with scope or complexity
- Gas fitting license not mentioned or expired
- Safety record not highlighted
- Team supervision or apprenticeship training omitted
- Tools and equipment experience not listed
- Vague descriptions like "general plumbing work"

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, sectors, key strengths)
2. Licenses & Tickets (with numbers and expiry dates — critical)
3. Professional Experience (project type, scope, responsibilities, outcomes)
4. Key Projects (notable installations or complex work)
5. Education (trade qualification, apprenticeship completion)
6. Additional: Safety awards, special training, referee availability
