ATS (Applicant Tracking System) Best Practices for Resume Success

1. EXACT KEYWORD MATCHING
ATS systems scan for exact keywords from the job description. If the JD says "managed a team of 5", your resume must say "managed a team of 5" — not "supervised 5 people." Always mirror the exact phrasing used in the job posting. This is the single most important rule for passing ATS screening.

2. INDUSTRY CERTIFICATIONS
Include all relevant certifications in a dedicated "Certifications" section. For healthcare: First Aid, CPR, Manual Handling, NDIS Worker Screening, Working with Children Check, Medication Administration. For IT: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, CompTIA, Cisco. Place certifications near the top of your resume, ideally right after the summary.

3. YEARS OF EXPERIENCE
Many ATS filters have a minimum years requirement. Always state your years clearly: "5 years of experience in aged care" rather than implying it through dates. Use both total years and per-role years. The ATS looks for numeric patterns.

4. ACTION VERBS
Start every bullet point with a strong action verb: achieved, improved, reduced, increased, developed, implemented, coordinated, managed, delivered, facilitated, mentored, optimized, resolved, transformed. Avoid passive language like "was responsible for" or "duties included." Passive verbs weaken your score significantly.

5. QUANTIFIABLE ACHIEVEMENTS
Numbers are scanned by ATS systems as markers of impact. Examples:
- Reduced incident reports by 30% through improved protocols
- Managed a caseload of 15+ clients simultaneously
- Trained 20+ new staff members annually
- Maintained 100% compliance during audit inspections
- Increased client satisfaction scores from 3.8 to 4.6
- Reduced medication errors by 40% through double-check system

6. RESUME FORMATTING FOR ATS
- Use standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications
- Do NOT use columns, tables, text boxes, or graphics — ATS cannot parse them
- Use a clean, single-column layout with consistent font
- Save as PDF only if the job posting specifies PDF; otherwise use .docx
- Font size should be 10-12pt body, 14-16pt headers
- Avoid headers/footers — some ATS systems cannot read them
- Use bullet points (• or -) not custom symbols

7. ATS FILE SCREENING PROCESS
Step 1: Parse — system extracts text from your file. If parsing fails, your resume is rejected immediately.
Step 2: Keyword match — system scores against JD keywords (typically 50-70% weight).
Step 3: Experience filter — checks years, roles, education requirements.
Step 4: Ranking — your resume gets a score. Only top 10-25% proceed to a human.
Most candidates are rejected in Step 1 (parse failure) or Step 2 (low keyword score). Human review happens only after all automated steps.

8. COMMON ATS PITFALLS
- Using acronyms without spelling them out: write "NDIS (National Disability Insurance Scheme)" on first use
- Missing location details: include city and state for every role
- Gap in employment without explanation: ATS flags gaps over 6 months
- Overly creative formatting: fancy templates with graphics fail parsing
- No skills section: ATS specifically looks for a dedicated skills area
- Using images or logos: these cannot be parsed and may corrupt extraction
- PDF scanned from image: must be selectable text, not a scanned picture

9. ATS KEYWORD CATEGORIES
Must-have keywords: appear 3+ times in the job description (include these in your resume verbatim).
Nice-to-have keywords: appear 1-2 times in the JD (include if space permits).
Industry keywords: standard terms for your field (always include these).
Soft skills keywords: communication, teamwork, leadership (use with evidence, not alone).

10. TAILORING FOR EACH APPLICATION
Do not send the same resume to every job. For each role:
- Identify 10-15 keywords from the job description
- Ensure those exact words appear in your resume
- Reorder bullet points to match the JD's priority order
- Adjust your summary to mirror the JD's language
- Remove irrelevant experience if it dilutes keyword density

11. ATS SCORING BREAKDOWN
Typically ATS scores are weighted as:
- Keyword match (exact + synonym): 50%
- Experience relevance (years + role fit): 25%
- Education match: 15%
- Formatting & readability: 10%
A score of 80%+ usually guarantees a human review. 60-79% may proceed depending on applicant volume. Below 60% is typically auto-rejected.

12. HYBRID ROLES AND ATS
For hybrid roles (e.g., healthcare IT, aged care management), create separate keyword lists for each domain. Your resume must satisfy both. Example: "Managed implementation of electronic health records system" satisfies both healthcare AND IT keywords simultaneously.
