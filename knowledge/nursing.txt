# Nursing Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on nursing resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Patient ratios and caseload sizes (e.g., "Managed 12-bed ward independently")
- Clinical competency breadth (e.g., "Proficient in wound care, medication admin, and palliative care")
- Audit and compliance results (e.g., "100% compliance in NSQHS standards audit")
- Continuing Professional Development (CPD) hours recorded
- Specialty qualifications or post-graduate study
- Preceptor/mentoring experience (e.g., "Preceptored 6 new graduate RNs")

## Metrics That Impress Nurse Managers
- Patient satisfaction scores and improvements
- Incident reduction rates (falls, medication errors, pressure injuries)
- Number of beds/patients managed per shift
- Wound healing rates or pressure injury prevention stats
- Training delivered to colleagues (e.g., "Delivered manual handling training to 30 staff")
- Quality improvement project outcomes (e.g., "Reduced catheter-associated UTIs by 25%")
- Team coordination (e.g., "Coordinated discharge planning for 15 patients/week")

## Critical Keywords to Check For
### Clinical Skills
- Medication administration (oral, IV, IM, SC), medication calculations
- Wound assessment and dressing, aseptic technique
- Vital signs monitoring, pain assessment, BGL
- Catheter care, stoma care, PEG care, tracheostomy care
- Manual handling, falls risk assessment, pressure area care

### Specialty Areas
- Aged care: dementia care, palliative care, ACFI/AN-ACC
- Critical care: ventilator management, arterial lines, CPAP/BiPAP
- Emergency: triage, resuscitation, trauma assessment
- Mental health: risk assessment, de-escalation, therapeutic communication
- Perioperative: scrub, scout, recovery, anaesthetic nursing

### Regulatory & Compliance
- AHPRA registration, NMBA standards, Code of Conduct
- NSQHS Standards, Aged Care Quality Standards
- Clinical governance, incident reporting, open disclosure
- Scope of practice, delegation, professional boundaries

## Common Resume Mistakes in Nursing
- Listing clinical skills without demonstrating competency level or application
- Missing AHPRA registration number or endorsement details
- No evidence of CPD or professional development activities
- Failing to show progression (e.g., EN → RN → CNS → NUM)
- Omitting shift coordination or team leadership experience
- Not mentioning specialty certifications (ALS, PALS, Immunisation)
- Using vague language instead of specific clinical scenarios

## Certifications & Licenses to Check
- AHPRA Registration (RN, EN, or Nurse Practitioner) — include registration number
- CPR/BLS (current — within 12 months)
- ALS (Advanced Life Support) for acute/critical care roles
- Immunisation Certificate for community health roles
- Venepuncture/Cannulation Certificate where applicable
- Triage Certificate for emergency roles
- Mentor/Preceptor qualifications

## Resume Structure Recommendations for Nursing
1. Professional Summary (years experience, clinical specialty, key strengths)
2. Clinical Competencies (categorized: Clinical Skills, Communication, Leadership)
3. Professional Experience (with quantified patient outcomes and team coordination)
4. Education (nursing degree, post-graduate qualifications)
5. Certifications & Registrations (AHPRA #, BLS, ALS, specialty certs — with expiry dates)
6. Professional Development (CPD hours, conferences, in-services)
7. Additional: Languages, Volunteer Work, Professional Memberships
