# Electrician Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on electrician resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Licenses and tickets with expiry dates (Electrical License, A Grade, White Card)
- Project scope (e.g., "Wired 200+ apartment complex from slab to fit-off")
- Safety record (e.g., "Zero safety incidents over 5 years on commercial sites")
- Supervision and apprentice mentoring (e.g., "Supervised 3 apprentices across multiple sites")
- Specialisation areas (domestic, commercial, industrial, solar, data)
- Testing and inspection experience with outcomes

## Metrics That Impress Electrical Contractors
- Number of projects completed (with scope details)
- Team leadership — apprentices or trades assistants supervised
- Job completion rates on time and on budget
- Safety incident record
- Client retention or repeat business
- Switchboard or complex installation counts

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Licenses & Tickets
- Electrical License (A Grade, B Grade)
- Electrical Fitter License (if applicable)
- White Card / Construction Induction
- High Risk Work License (EWP, forklift)
- Solar Accreditation (Clean Energy Council)
- Fire Panel, Data Cabling, Security System endorsements
- First Aid, CPR, Working at Heights, Confined Space
- Test and Tag qualification

### Experience Types
- Domestic: new builds, renovations, fault finding, switchboard upgrades
- Commercial: fit-outs, data cabling, lighting control, fire systems
- Industrial: motor control, PLC, HV switching, instrumentation
- Solar/PV: grid-connected, battery storage, design and installation
- Maintenance: breakdown response, preventative maintenance

### Technical Skills
- Switchboard building and installation
- Cable tray and conduit installation
- Earth fault loop impedance testing
- RCD testing and tagging
- Metering installation (smart meters)
- Electrical testing and commissioning

## Common Resume Mistakes for Electricians
- License number or expiry date missing
- No project examples with scope or value
- Safety record not mentioned
- Team supervision experience omitted
- Training or apprentice mentoring not highlighted
- Tools and testing equipment not listed
- Vague descriptions like "general electrical work"

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, sectors, key strengths)
2. Licenses & Tickets (with numbers and expiry dates — critical)
3. Professional Experience (project type, scope, responsibilities, outcomes)
4. Key Projects (notable installations or complex work)
5. Education (trade qualification, apprenticeship completion)
6. Additional: Safety awards, special training, referee availability
