# Construction Resume Evaluation Guidance
# Use this to evaluate and provide feedback on construction resumes.

## Key Resume Strengths to Look For
- Project scope and value (e.g., "Managed $5M commercial construction project")
- Safety record (e.g., "Zero lost-time injuries over 500,000 hours worked")
- Licenses and tickets (White Card, High-Risk Work License, relevant tickets)
- Team leadership numbers (e.g., "Supervised crew of 20+ tradespeople")
- Budget and timeline management (e.g., "Completed project 2 weeks ahead of schedule")
- Contractor and supplier management experience

## Metrics That Impress Construction Managers
- Project completion rates on time and under budget
- Safety incident rates and improvement trends
- Number of projects managed simultaneously
- Team sizes supervised across trades
- Client satisfaction or repeat business rates
- Cost savings through alternative methods or materials

## Key Areas to Evaluate
### Licenses & Tickets
- Construction Induction (White Card / Blue Card)
- High Risk Work License (forklift, crane, boom, dogging, rigging)
- Trade-specific licenses (electrical, plumbing, gas)
- Operating tickets (excavator, skid steer, roller)
- First Aid, CPR, Working at Heights, Confined Space

### Experience Types
- Residential vs commercial vs civil construction
- New build vs renovation vs fit-out
- Project role: labourer → tradesperson → supervisor → project manager
- Subcontractor management and coordination
- Government/infrastructure project experience

### Management Skills
- Site safety and WHS/OHS implementation
- Quality control and defect management
- Procurement and material ordering
- Progress claims and reporting
- Contract administration

## Common Resume Mistakes in Construction
- No specific project examples with values and timelines
- Missing safety ticket numbers or expiry dates
- Vague about role on projects — "worked on" without scope of responsibility
- No team leadership or coordination evidence
- Safety record not mentioned
- Tools and equipment experience not listed

## Resume Structure Recommendations
1. Professional Summary (years experience, project types, role level)
2. Licenses & Tickets (with ticket numbers and expiry dates)
3. Professional Experience (project name, value, role, responsibilities, outcomes)
4. Key Projects (portfolio of notable projects with details)
5. Education (trade qualification, certifications)
6. Additional: Safety awards, professional memberships
